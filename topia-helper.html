📒 TOPIA Helper

One bookmark for TOPIA: copy students, fill the Weekly Report, bring in online homework. Set it up once per device. Everything is inside the bookmark: nothing is loaded from anywhere else.

💻 Computer (Chrome, Edge, Whale, Safari)

  1. Drag this button onto your bookmarks bar (no bar? press Ctrl+Shift+B): 📒 TOPIA Helper
  2. On any TOPIA page, click it.

📱 iPad / iPhone (Safari)

  1. Copied
  2. Tap Share (□↑) → Add Bookmark → name it TOPIA Helper → Save.
  3. Open Bookmarks (📖) → Edit → tap TOPIA Helper, replace the address with what you copied, tap Done.
  4. On TOPIA, tap the address bar, type TOPIA Helper and tap the bookmark.

🤖 Android tablet / phone (Chrome)

  1. Copied
  2. Tap ⋮ → ☆ to bookmark this page, then Edit.
  3. Name it TOPIA Helper, paste over the URL, save.
  4. On TOPIA, type TOPIA Helper in the address bar and tap the bookmark.
